FIELD NOTES / ПОЛЕВЫЕ ЗАМЕТКИ

One repeated structure. Countless individual worlds. This imagined apartment block moves from the scale of the district to the small rituals of home: a stair rail polished by hands, a carpet kept on the wall, a kitchen table doing more than one job.

A fully three-dimensional architectural interpretation of the supplied references. The exterior follows the taller Brezhnevka-era character of the photograph; the interiors bring together details of Soviet-era domestic life. This is an artistic composite, not a measured reconstruction or a historical identification.

REFERENCE IMAGES — supplied for this project. Original photographers and building identification unverified.
SPATIAL STUDY — one fully modeled environment, rendered with dense surface-aligned Gaussian splats and structural meshes. These are procedurally generated Gaussian splats, not a trained photogrammetry dataset. Every splat has a world-space position and two surface axes. Guided and free navigation use the same scene. The photographs appear only in these reference notes.
CONTROLS — scroll / numbered spaces / arrow buttons. Explore: orbit, pan, zoom, WASD or touch arrows. Escape resumes the guided route. M toggles sound.
RECONSTRUCTION NOTES / ИСТОЧНИКИ

Three modeled domestic rooms: living room, bedroom and 3 × 2 m kitchen. A representative late-Soviet interior within a taller panel-block exterior, rather than a measured reconstruction of one building or series. Surface textures are procedural; the Gaussian field is generated from the 3D geometry, not trained from photographs.

Historical reference: Lida Museum — furniture and the Khrushchyovka interior · Energy and Technology Museum — apartment exhibition.
